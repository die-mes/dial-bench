# Dial Bench

Watch dial designer for Seiko mods (NH35, NH36, 4R36, 7S26, 7S36, VK67) with laser-ready exports.

Fonts: Google Fonts families via Fontsource, latin subset, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 or Apache License 2.0 as published by each family.
